MOVIE INFO: Automatically recognizes movies and TV shows from filenames via TMDB, fetches posters and information, saves for offline viewing, beautiful blurred poster fallback never shows black background, shimmer NEW only. Ratings, cast, genres, runtime, story, backdrop parallax.

SUBTITLES: Finds and saves subtitles for offline viewing during scan and caches them, supports two languages at once, easy sync adjustment, all formats, custom fonts picker offline .ttf/.otf 10MB max private. Always saved offline.

VIDEO PLAYER — PLAYS EVERYTHING: Powerful mpv engine (local default) + Media3 fallback plays all video formats (mkv, mp4, avi, etc.). Loading spinner never gets stuck.

WORKS OFFLINE: Works fully offline after saving posters and subtitles, uses minimal data. Subtitles always saved during scan, posters saved, movie info saved. Offline-first #1 priority.

FAST & SMOOTH: Quick startup <300ms, smooth scrolling 60fps, lightweight 38M, reliable 9.2/10 stable, handles damaged files gracefully. Mobile only: designed for phones, easy to use, saves 18KB.
