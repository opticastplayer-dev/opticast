FAQ — quick answers

Is it for TV?
No — designed for phones. Works on Android 8 and newer, including older devices. Focused on providing the best phone experience.
Does it work offline?
Yes — works fully offline after the initial setup. Your videos, posters, and subtitles are saved to your device, so you can watch without an internet connection.
How to update?
Install the new version over the old one — your data is preserved. You can also update directly inside the app from Settings → Check for updates.
Why does it need install permission?
To allow in-app updates. When a new version is available, you can download and install it directly from the app. Installation always requires your confirmation in the system installer.
Where are my videos?
OptiCast looks in your Movies, DCIM, and Download folders. If a video is missing, check Settings → Excluded folders to ensure the folder is not excluded, then pull to refresh in your Library.
