OptiCast — Your Local Cinema

Best local video player for Android — Infuse alternative, free movie player offline, no ads, open source GPL-3.0

OptiCast is a beautiful, performance-focused local video player for Android — inspired by Infuse. Scans device, auto-identifies movies & TV shows, fetches posters from TMDB, subtitles from OpenSubtitles/SubDL, plays with mpv + Media3 fallback.

✨ Features

  • Library: MediaStore auto-scan, adaptive poster grid, Continue Watching, Movies/TV grouping, Favorites, Recently Added
  • Player: mpv + Media3, 31M official signed release with full mpv, libmpv.so 6.1M + libavcodec 12M
  • Performance: Buttery smooth scrolling, 16/48 MiB cache, baseline profiles, R8 optimizations, low-RAM 32-bit 3GB fast
  • Updates: In-app download & install, Up To Date notification when matches GitHub, What's New card
  • No ads, no tracking, GPL-3.0
