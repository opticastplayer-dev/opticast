OptiCast — Your videos, beautifully organized.

Get a great local video player experience with OptiCast! It finds and organizes your Movies and TV shows with posters and info, and plays them flawlessly offline

Why it's different:
Most players try to do everything. OptiCast focuses on one thing: playing your own videos well. Focused local playback, no streaming catalogs, no login walls. Scans your device via MediaStore, shows Continue Watching, Recently Added, Collections. Fast, even on older phones.

Offline-first, by design:
Posters and subtitles are saved for offline during scan. Works fully offline after first scan. Update check once when internet detected, minimal data.

Private & simple:
No ads, no tracking, GPL-3.0. Your media stays on device. No accounts. Small APK, manual DI, no bloat. Easy to audit.

LIBRARY:
Finds your videos automatically, adaptive poster grid (Compact/Comfortable), Continue Watching, Movies & TV Shows, Favorites, Recently Added, Collections, search. Fast scrolling. If you don't see videos, check Settings → Excluded folders.

MOVIE INFO:
Recognizes movies/TV from filenames, posters cached offline, blurred fallback never black. Cast, genres, runtime.

SUBTITLES:
OpenSubtitles + SubDL, multi-language, always saved offline during scan, custom fonts .ttf/.otf, dual subs.

PLAYER:
mpv + Media3 fallback, plays mkv/mp4/avi and more, edge-to-edge, gestures for seek/volume/brightness, speed, aspect, audio/subs picker, predictive back, sleep timer, auto-play next, chapters, PiP auto-resumes.

UPDATES:
Checks once when internet detected, minimal data, in-app download with progress via FileProvider, full changelog visible, Up To Date only when update available.

GETTING STARTED (30 seconds):
1. Install APK, grant video permission
2. Open — auto-scans videos
3. Tap poster — watch, posters cached offline

Where are my videos? Looks in Movies, DCIM, Download via MediaStore. If missing: Settings → Excluded folders → remove exclusion, pull to refresh. Files hidden (dot) or in Android/data are hidden by system.

PRIVATE:
No ads, no tracking, GPL-3.0, media stays on device, no accounts. Permissions: Internet, Network State, Video Library, Notifications, Background Playback, Install Updates.
