Why it's different

Most players try to do everything. OptiCast focuses on one thing: playing your own videos well.

🎬 Focused local playback

No streaming catalogs, no login walls. It scans your device with MediaStore, matches titles locally, and shows Continue Watching, Recently Added, Collections. Fast, even on low-RAM devices.

📴 Offline-first, by design

After first scan, posters and subtitles are saved for offline. Works fully offline. Update check happens once when internet is detected, not constantly — minimal data use.

🔒 Private & simple

No ads, no tracking, GPL-3.0. Your media stays on your device. No accounts. Small APK, manual DI, no bloat. Easy to audit.

Getting Started — 30 seconds

1
Install
Download APK from GitHub Releases and install. Allow video permission.
2
Open
OptiCast auto-scans your videos. If you don't see them, check Settings → Folders — maybe a folder is excluded.
3
Watch
Tap a poster. Posters and subtitles are fetched and cached for offline.
Where are my videos? — OptiCast looks in your Movies, DCIM, Download folders via MediaStore. If missing: Settings → Excluded folders → remove exclusion, then Library → pull to refresh. Also check Android's Files app — if file is hidden (starts with dot) or in Android/data, system hides it.

First-run experience — audited

  • Permission: On first open, explains why video permission needed (to find your videos), then system dialog.
  • Empty state: If no videos, shows friendly message + button to open folder settings + tooltip "Where are my videos?"
  • Quick tour: 3-dot tooltip on first library open: swipe for search, long-press for options, pull to refresh. Dismissible, not intrusive.
  • Tooltips: Settings → Excluded folders has helper text. Library → if filtered empty, shows why.
  • Updates: Checks once when internet detected, not every 6h. Shows "Up to Date" only when real update available, dismissible X 48dp.
